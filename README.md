Ashbourne Industries – 2024 Trial Balance Audit

Career Catalyst: Audit work. This project checks Ashbourne Industries, Inc.'s 2024 monthly trial balance and reports what it finds.

Key finding
January to November balance to within $1–$8 (rounding).
December is out of balance by (97,403,376). That equals the year's net income, which was booked to Retained Earnings while the revenue and expense accounts stayed open.
